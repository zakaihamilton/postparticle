# Editorial design

The primary app uses Source Sans 3 for interface text and Lora for editorial headings, with white surfaces, light blue accents, and dark navy text. Fonts are self-hosted by next/font. Lucide icons are imported explicitly behind the existing Icon component; the brand mark remains bespoke.

The light palette uses white backgrounds, `#f3f8fd` secondary surfaces, `#eaf5ff` highlights, `#d8e6f2` borders, `#182c40` text, and `#526b80` muted text. Primary actions use `#b9dcff` with navy labels and a `#a4d0f9` hover fill. Links, icons, and focus indicators use `#245b87`. Dark and system-dark themes share navy surfaces and blue accents. Green success and red error colors remain semantic. Typography, imagery, layout, and motion remain unchanged.

Motion tokens: 140ms for feedback, 200ms for overlays, 350ms for entrances. Motion follows state changes and respects prefers-reduced-motion. Demo publishing never writes real articles.

## Generated assets

These fictional editorial photographs were generated with the built-in image tool, visually inspected, and converted to 1536 × 1024 WebP at quality 85. Original assets remain available. Generated source PNGs remain in the tool's output directory.

### studio

- Asset: `public/images/welcome/studio-editorial.webp`
- Tool: built-in image generation
- Prompt:

> Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: editorial photography for Postparticle publishing app. Primary request: An independent editor's working desk in a quiet Mediterranean publishing studio. A worn pale oak desk, blank cream paper proofs, closed linen-bound books, black pencil, ceramic coffee cup, olive foliage near a window. Candid oblique view, natural imperfect arrangement, usable negative space. No laptop screens. Style: authentic magazine photography, subtle fine film grain, realistic optics and material texture, soft natural light. Palette: warm ivory, stone, muted olive, charcoal, desaturated blue-gray. Landscape 3:2, 1536x1024. Avoid: text, letters, logos, watermarks, UI, CGI, glossy 3D, oversaturation, fantasy light, overly perfect symmetry.
